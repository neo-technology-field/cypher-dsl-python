# NNNN: <short decision title>

- Status: proposed.

## Context

- What forces this decision now.
- The constraint, measurement or failed alternative that makes the choice necessary.
- What happens if the decision is deferred.

## Decision

- The choice, in the present tense, with the concrete setting, version or boundary.
- The parts a contributor must follow, not only the parts they may find interesting.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier and what becomes harder or more expensive.
- The follow-up work this creates and where it is tracked.

## Alternatives considered

- Each serious alternative and the specific reason it lost.
- Name the cost of the chosen option in the same list, so the record is not a sales pitch.
